Our Offerings

What is outsourced accounting?

Outsourced accounting refers to a firm keeping your books, closing your month and preparing your reports, so you do not need an in-house team.

  • Monthly close and reconciliation
  • Accounts payable and receivable
  • Financial statements for lenders

Retirement Plan Audit Support

According to the U.S. Department of Labor, plans with 100 or more participants need an annual audit. We prepare the records your auditor asks for.

  1. Step 1: We collect the plan documents.
  2. Step 2: We reconcile contributions with payroll.
  3. Step 3: We answer the auditor's questions.
