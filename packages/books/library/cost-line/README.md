# The Cost Line

The pilot of **Business as Usual**, a dark corporate satire. Outside, the Burn years: fires, floods and rolling blackouts. Inside Ardent Consolidated, the quarterly close goes on, the org chart holds, and anyone whose work makes the gaps above them visible gets a quiet warning. Elias Rourke builds the finance systems and the AI agents that keep the whole thing honest, until the market decides he is a cost line.

Everything here is fiction: the company, the city, the people and the events are invented. The satire is aimed at systems — the market's arithmetic, the incentives, the org chart — never at an identifiable person.

## Before you write

1. Read [`outline.md`](outline.md), then the act guide covering your chapter, then the chapters either side of it.
2. Check [`notes/characters.md`](notes/characters.md) and [`notes/continuity.md`](notes/continuity.md), including the content rules.
3. To work with the AI, follow [`guides/ai-prompts.md`](guides/ai-prompts.md): every result arrives as a suggestion you accept or reject.
4. Run `books validate cost-line` when you are done.
