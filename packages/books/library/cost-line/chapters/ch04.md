## Outline and Contextual Alignment

### Purpose of the chapter

- Show how a valuable role threatens incompetent leadership: not by accusing, just by reconciling.
- The first quiet threat, delivered as praise.

### Beats

1. Ledger goes live: it compares what the company said (forecasts, board decks) with what the company did (cash, invoices, journals).
2. It finds that a forecast Haldane presented two quarters running never reconciled to anything.
3. Elias raises it in the right meeting, in the right tone, with no names.
4. Haldane thanks him warmly, calls it a great catch, and moves Ledger's output to a dashboard only Haldane can see.
5. Priya notices that the finding has disappeared from the team's view. Marisol notices that she was copied, then uncopied.
6. Elias is given a 'stretch assignment' that takes him out of the forecasting process.

### Hook

Haldane, over coffee: 'Great catch. Let's keep this one close.' Elias understands it is not about the forecast.
