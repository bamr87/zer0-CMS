## Outline and Contextual Alignment

### Purpose of the chapter

- Turn fatherhood into method: year-scale growth from daily inputs as a way to learn and ship.
- Elias's finance-systems expertise and AI agents become his own edge.

### Beats

1. 4:50 to 5:40 is build time; 5:40 is June; the rest of the day is small, shippable things.
2. He rebuilds an agent from scratch, his own, without anything that belongs to Ardent.
3. He sells reconciliations by the hour to small firms that cannot afford a finance team.
4. June learns a new word every day and a new sentence every week; he keeps a log of both, and of what he shipped.
5. A daily deposit is small enough to survive a bad day; he stops waiting for big ones.

### Hook

A small client asks whether his agent could audit a company the size of Ardent.
