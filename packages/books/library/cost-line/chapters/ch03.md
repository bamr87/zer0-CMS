## Outline and Contextual Alignment

### Purpose of the chapter

- Plant the heart of the book: fatherhood, and the schedule that must survive.
- Show the collision between a child's time and the quarter's time.

### Beats

1. June is born on day three of a quarter-end close; Elias answers a reconciliation question from the hospital corridor and hates himself precisely.
2. The first months: Nadia's shifts, the blur, the bottle at 2:00 and 4:00.
3. By four months June settles on 5:40 a.m. and keeps it. Elias stops fighting it and builds around it: up at 4:50, build until 5:40, then June.
4. Haldane praises his 'commitment' in a team meeting and schedules the next close review for 6:00 a.m.
5. June's growth measured in year-scale leaps from tiny daily inputs; Elias starts thinking about systems that learn the same way.

### Hook

At 5:39 one morning he sketches the first version of Ledger on the back of a daycare form.
