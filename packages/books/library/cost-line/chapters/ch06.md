## Outline and Contextual Alignment

### Purpose of the chapter

- Return to the present after the cut.
- The theme of pricing yourself by someone else's number.

### Beats

1. The first Monday without a badge. June still wakes at 5:40; she does not know he has nowhere to be.
2. Recruiters, salary surveys, comp bands and a separation formula each tell Elias what he is worth. None of the numbers agree, and none is his.
3. A job board's AI ranks him against thousands of other cut roles from the same quarter.
4. Nadia asks the only question that matters: what does he want to build.
5. He realises the market priced his role, not him, and that he had been letting it price him for years.

### Hook

Priya messages: Ledger is still running inside Ardent, and it just found something new.
