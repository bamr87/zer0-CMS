## Outline and Contextual Alignment

### Purpose of the chapter

- The first flashback: the wild ride of making and moving mountains for a large organisation.
- Show Elias at his best and the company at its most honest about needing him.

### Beats

1. Elias's first week: the quarterly close takes nineteen days and depends on a spreadsheet older than the CFO, maintained by one man who is retiring.
2. He maps every ledger, every feed, every manual journal entry; Marisol Teague shows him where the bodies are buried, procedurally speaking.
3. The rebuild: nights, a failed cutover, a weekend war room, a close in eleven days, then eight, then six.
4. He hires Priya Venn, who is faster than he is and tells him so.
5. Haldane, newly arrived, presents the six-day close to the board as his own; Elias lets it go because the system works and that is what he cares about.
6. Marisol keeps a private file of everything she signs.

### Hook

Elias is promoted and given a team. He believes good systems protect the people who build them.
