## Outline and Contextual Alignment

### Purpose of the chapter

- Business as usual during catastrophe, at its most extreme.
- The books are adjusted without anyone saying so; the gap above Elias becomes a chasm.

### Beats

1. The Burn closes the port for nine weeks; floodwater reaches the lobby. Desmond Okoro sandbags the doors and fuels the generators.
2. Upstairs, the leadership offsite proceeds on schedule, with a session on 'agility'.
3. Pell releases reserves to make the quarter and calls it 'normalising'. Marisol signs, and adds a page to her file.
4. Elias's agents flag the release; Haldane calls the flag 'noise from the model'.
5. At home, the power is out for three days; June's 5:40 survives by flashlight.
6. Elias is added to a 'transformation' steering committee without being told what is being transformed.

### Hook

The committee's first agenda item is a list of roles, and his team is on it, highlighted in a colour the committee chose.
