# Act II — The Gaps (chapters 4–6)

> Check [`../notes/continuity.md`](../notes/continuity.md) (content rules, timeline) and [`../notes/characters.md`](../notes/characters.md) before writing.

## Summary

The gaps become visible and the cost of visibility arrives. Chapters 4 and 5 are the last two flashbacks; chapter 6 rejoins the present a week after the cut.

## Group goals

- Show how a valuable role threatens incompetent leadership: not by accusing anyone, just by reconciling.
- Show the quiet threats: praise with conditions, reorgs, "visibility" as a career risk.
- Show business as usual during catastrophe: the port closes, the offsite goes ahead.
- Show the cost of pricing yourself by someone else's number.

## Per-chapter beats

- **ch04 — The Reconciliation (minus 2 years).** Ledger finds that a forecast Haldane presented never reconciled; Elias raises it in the right meeting the right way; Haldane thanks him warmly and moves Ledger's output to a dashboard only Haldane can see. Hook: "Great catch. Let's keep this one close."
- **ch05 — Restatement Weather (minus 1 year).** The Burn closes the port for nine weeks; Desmond sandbags the lobby; upstairs, Pell releases reserves to make the quarter and nobody says what that means; Marisol signs; Elias's agents flag it and he is told the flag is "noise". Hook: Elias is added to a "transformation" steering committee; he does not yet know what is being transformed.
- **ch06 — Price Discovery (Day 0 plus 1 week).** Recruiters, comp bands, salary surveys and a separation formula all tell Elias what he is worth; he notices none of them are his number. June does not care what he is worth. Hook: Priya messages that Ledger is still running and found something new.

## Rules

- No money figures for Elias's exit, ever. Describe the formula, not the amount.
- The threats are always polite.
