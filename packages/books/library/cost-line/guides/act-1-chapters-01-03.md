# Act I — The Crash and the Climb (chapters 1–3)

> Check [`../notes/continuity.md`](../notes/continuity.md) (content rules, timeline) and [`../notes/characters.md`](../notes/characters.md) before writing.

## Summary

Open at the crash, then go back to the beginning. Chapter 1 is the present day of the cut; chapters 2 and 3 are flashbacks six and four years earlier. The act moves from absurd dread to the energy of building, and lands on the fixed point of the book: June's 5:40 a.m.

## Group goals

- Establish the frame: the world outside is ending, the tower inside is running a quarterly close.
- Establish Elias's edge: he understands the money's plumbing and builds agents that read it.
- Establish the rule: making gaps visible is valued on paper and punished in practice.
- Establish the heart: June's schedule is the one thing that is not negotiable.

## Per-chapter beats

- **ch01 — Cash Flow Imperative (Day 0).** The all-hands; the mandate nobody can parse; the finance meeting where the books get cooked without the word; Ledger's finding; the 11:45 calendar invite; the cut. Hook: the stock ticks up as he carries his box past the lobby screen.
- **ch02 — Moving Mountains (minus 6 to minus 5 years).** The nineteen-day close; a spreadsheet older than the CFO; the wild ride of rebuilding it; Haldane takes the credit at the board and Elias lets him. Hook: Marisol Teague's private file.
- **ch03 — 5:40 (minus 4 years).** June's birth during a quarter-end; the first night shift; the wake-up that sets the schedule; Elias starts building at 4:50 so he can be done by 5:40. Hook: the idea for Ledger, born at 5:39.

## Rules

- Every chapter's `era` is "The Burn years"; the relative time goes in the subtitle.
- The narration never uses a buzzword sincerely.
