# Act III — Forward Guidance (chapters 7–8)

> Check [`../notes/continuity.md`](../notes/continuity.md) (content rules, timeline) and [`../notes/characters.md`](../notes/characters.md) before writing.

## Summary

The present and the near future. Elias rebuilds on June's clock, with his skills and his agents as his edge, and the book ends looking forward — at his prospects, at Ardent's next quarter, at the city.

## Group goals

- Turn fatherhood into method: a child's year-scale growth built from daily inputs becomes how Elias learns and ships.
- Avoid a triumphant ending. The market does not apologise; the city does not recover. Elias changes the unit he measures himself in.
- Leave threads open for the series: Ardent, Priya, Marisol, the Burn.

## Per-chapter beats

- **ch07 — Daily Deposits (plus 3 to 6 months).** 4:50 to 5:40 is build time; 5:40 is June; days are small shipments. He rebuilds an agent from scratch and sells its findings by the hour. Hook: a small client asks if his agent can audit a company like Ardent.
- **ch08 — Forward Guidance (plus about 1 year).** Ardent's earnings call promises the market a future; Elias watches with June on his lap; Port Calder floods again; he names his own number. Close on 5:40 a.m.

## Rules

- End in the same plain voice as the opening. No speeches.
