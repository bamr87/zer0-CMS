# Character bible — The Cost Line

The single place a name, a role or a relationship gets decided. Every name here is invented; none stands for a real person. Add a row before you use a name in prose, and add it to `cast` in `book.yml`.

## Principals

| Name | Role | First appears | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Elias Rourke | Protagonist. Director of finance systems at Ardent Consolidated; builds the close engine and its AI agents. Late thirties. | ch01 | Plain-spoken, dry, exact. Thinks in reconciliations: what was said against what happened. Never uses jargon sincerely. |
| June Rourke | Elias's daughter. Two and a half in ch01; born in ch03. | ch01 | Wakes at 5:40 a.m. without fail. Her growth is measured in years and built from days. |
| Nadia Rourke | Elias's wife. A pediatric nurse working Burn-season shifts. | ch01 | The adult who sees through him fastest. Not a prop: she has her own exhaustion and her own triage. |
| Ledger | Elias's reconciliation agent; the first of his AI agents. | ch01 | Speaks in short findings. Has no opinions, only differences. Never anthropomorphised past a dry habit of footnotes. |
