# Continuity ledger — The Cost Line

Settled facts, the timeline, the content rules and open questions. A fact only counts as settled once it is written here.

## Content rules

These hold for every chapter and every AI-assisted draft.

- **Fiction only.** The company, city, people and events are invented. Do not name or allude to any real employer, real person, real place or real date that would identify one.
- **No exit paperwork or payout figures.** No contracts, payouts or money figures attached to the protagonist's exit. The cut is described by what it does, not what it pays.
- **Satire targets systems.** The market's arithmetic, incentives, the org chart. No character is a portrait of an identifiable person.
- **No sincere buzzwords.** Corporate language appears only in characters' mouths or on slides, and the narration never adopts it.
