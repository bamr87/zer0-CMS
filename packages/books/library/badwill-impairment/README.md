# Badwill Impairment: Corporate Cache Compromised

A financial thriller. A laid-off systems engineer finds that the goodwill impairment that cost him his job covered the transfer of his company's technology and data to a foreign military regime, through systems he built.

A fresh draft from the premise of [The Impairment](../impairment/README.md): its premise, cast and open questions carry over, its chapters do not. Fourteen chapters across three acts, outlined and not yet written, against a 90,000-word target.

## Before you write

1. Read [`outline.md`](outline.md), then the chapter's own outline and the chapters either side of it.
2. Check [`notes/characters.md`](notes/characters.md) for any name you are about to use. If it is not there, decide it and add it.
3. Answer the open questions in [`notes/continuity.md`](notes/continuity.md) that the chapter depends on, and record the answer there first.
4. Run `books validate badwill-impairment` when you are done.

## What the first draft taught

The Impairment's [editorial review](../../docs/reviews/impairment.md) found a draft written ahead of its own decisions: the company and its CFO never named, the layoff staged twice, a first-person opening in a third-person book, and outline notes left in the prose. This draft decides first: names before scenes, each event staged once, notes kept out of the prose.
