**"Badwill Impairment: Corporate Cache Compromised"** *(working titles carried from the first draft: The Impairment, Goodwill, The Valuation)*

A fresh draft from the premise of [The Impairment](../impairment/outline.md). That draft's chapters stay where they are; this book reuses its premise, cast and open questions and starts the prose again. Everything below is a seed for the author to change.

---

## Synopsis

Kareem Hassan is a senior systems engineer and naturalized immigrant at a century-old American manufacturer in the country's manufacturing belt, a company proud of never having laid anyone off. He builds the systems that run its plants and keep its data, and he integrated a large, fast-tracked equipment order from Al-Madina Holdings, a government-linked buyer in his country of origin.

After the long-serving CEO is pushed out, a chief financial officer from private equity takes over and closes a bold international acquisition. The company later writes most of that deal off as a goodwill impairment. The stock falls, and the first layoff in the company's history takes Kareem with it.

Going through a backup he kept of his own work, Kareem finds that the write-down was the visible half of a transaction. The value that vanished from his company's books reappeared on someone else's as badwill, the gain a buyer records when it pays less than assets are worth, and the company's designs and data, its corporate cache, left through the systems he built. The buyers answer to a military regime, and the equipment his work helped ship is poisoning the Tarna Valley at home.

Caught between two countries, two identities and a legacy collapsing under corruption, Kareem must decide whether to expose what he found, at a cost to the family the regime can still reach.

## What the title says

- **Goodwill impairment.** What a company writes off when an acquisition turns out to be worth less than it paid. It is the event that ends Kareem's career.
- **Badwill.** Negative goodwill: the gain a buyer books when it pays less than the assets are worth. Here it is the other side of the same deal. One ledger's write-down is another's bargain; the value did not disappear, it moved.
- **Corporate cache.** What a company keeps in its systems: designs, configurations, plant data. Said aloud, *cache* is *cash*, and both leave with the deal.
- **Compromised.** What happened to that cache, and to the people who kept it.

## Key Themes

The canonical list is in [`book.yml`](book.yml); these are carried from The Impairment.

1. **Economic colonialism.** Corporations acting as agents of empire, using financial instruments to exploit developing nations.
2. **Corporate dehumanization.** People reduced to costs and liabilities; history and legacy treated as spreadsheet cells.
3. **Identity and guilt.** An immigrant and a corporate insider, carrying the weight of both worlds.
4. **Environmental injustice.** The damage done far from the boardroom that ordered it.
5. **Weaponization of finance.** "Goodwill", "impairment" and "badwill" as words that hide statecraft.
6. **Moral conflict.** Expose the truth and risk everything, or stay complicit.
7. **Personal sacrifice.** What resistance costs a family.
8. **Legacy and betrayal.** A proud company's name, spent on someone else's war.

## Narrative Structure

Three acts, fourteen chapters. Each chapter file holds its beats under "Outline and Contextual Alignment"; this is the spine.

### Act I — The Legacy and the Lie (chapters 1–5)

The plant before anything breaks, the order that ties Kareem's work to home, the acquisition, the CFO's takeover, and the layoff, staged once, in chapter 5.

### Act II — The Breakdown (chapters 6–9)

The impairment goes public; Kareem's backup shows what the exported systems carried; Sarah finds the badwill on a buyer's books; Omar's letters bring the Tarna Valley onto the page.

### Act III — The Revelation and Reckoning (chapters 10–14)

The pattern assembled; the people behind the deal find Kareem; the choice; the record put together for Elena; the ending the author chooses.
