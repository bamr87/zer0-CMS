## Open questions

Ordered by how much of the draft each answer shapes. Answer them before the chapters that depend on them.

1. **What is the company called?** It is on the plant's signage, the ticker, the filing in ch06 and the record in ch13.
2. **What is the CFO called?** The antagonist cannot stay a job title.
3. **What is the acquisition target called, and how is it tied to the regime?**
4. **Who books the badwill?** Which buyer acquires which assets at a bargain (Al-Madina Holdings, the acquisition target, or a third party), and when.
5. **What is the cache?** What exactly is in Kareem's backup, and is it his to keep? The answer decides whether the reader sees a whistleblower or a thief.
6. **Is the homeland contact one person or two?** If Omar is also the contact Kareem calls in act I, he appears before ch09.
7. **First person or third?** The first draft opened in first person and continued in third. This draft keeps third person limited unless the author chooses otherwise before chapter one.
8. **How does it end?** Expose, disappear, or document (ch14).
