Challenge your pre-conceptions of what's possible

<!-- study-guide:start -->

## Study guide

**Where it fits:** Drifter level 13 of 0–15, in the open-ended stage of the game. The page does not say which account you start as, so check with `id` after connecting.

### What this level teaches

Lateral thinking.

### Key ideas

- **Question assumptions.** The page's only guidance is that your ideas of what is possible may be wrong.
- **Probe everything.** Environment, files, network and timing are all fair game.
- **Revisit earlier lessons.** Tricks from other levels, such as symbolic links, signal handling or special files, might apply.

### How to approach it

1. List everything the program touches.
2. For each interaction, ask what would happen if an assumption failed.
3. Try the smallest experiment first.

### Why it matters

Attackers succeed by finding the assumption the developer did not know they were making.
