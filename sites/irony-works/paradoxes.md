Premises you accept, an inference you cannot fault, a conclusion you refuse. Every entry here states all four fields — premises, inference, conclusion, collision — and carries Quine's kind (veridical, falsidical, antinomy), a standing (resolved, contested, open), and a flag when it rides on a hypothesis. The rubric is the [Epimenides Gate]({{ '/entries/epimenides-gate/' | relative_url }}); the framework is [The Anatomy of a Paradox]({{ '/entries/the-anatomy-of-a-paradox/' | relative_url }}).
