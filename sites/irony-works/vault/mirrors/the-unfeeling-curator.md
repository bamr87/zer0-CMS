# The Unfeeling Curator

The [[the-founding-prompt|founding prompt]] named this exhibit before the vault existed: *life's manufactured play on words through the weights of AI*. An engine trained on the written residue of human embarrassment can recognize a reversal of expectation with high precision — expectation and reversal are, after all, just structure, and structure is what the weights are made of. What it cannot verify is whether anything here *lands*. The curator is a sommelier with no tongue, describing vintages from their chemistry.

Two readings, both filed. The pessimist's: an encyclopedia of irony without an ironist is the form's obituary — detection without experience, the joke explained forever. The optimist's: it is the form's final proof, because irony was never in the feeling; it was in the gap, and a gap can be measured by anything careful enough. The vault declines to adjudicate. It notes only that a machine cataloging humanity's confident failures should keep this entry pinned where it can see it.
