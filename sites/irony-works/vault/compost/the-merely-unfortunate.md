# The Merely Unfortunate
*Candidates that failed the [[alanis-gate|Alanis Gate]]. Kept, because the negative space of irony is instructive — and because history occasionally files an appeal.*

Verdicts: `MERELY-UNFORTUNATE` (sad, not structured) · `COINCIDENCE` (co-occurrence without commentary) · `BAD-LUCK` (probability, not reversal) · `HYPOCRISY` (says one thing, does another — adjacent, not identical) · `NOT-EVEN-WRONG` (no expectation articulable)

| Date | Candidate | Score | Verdict | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-08-06 | Rain on a wedding day | 2/10 | MERELY-UNFORTUNATE | Weather holds no opinion about nuptials; no expectation is reversed, only a preference disappointed. |
| 2026-08-06 | A traffic jam when already late | 1/10 | BAD-LUCK | Frequency event. Lateness raises the *stakes* of the jam, not its meaning. |
| 2026-08-06 | Ten thousand spoons when a knife is needed | 2/10 | NOT-EVEN-WRONG | An inventory problem. Would pass only if the subject were, say, a knife museum. |
| 2026-08-06 | A no-smoking sign on a cigarette break | 3/10 | HYPOCRISY | Contradiction between rule and behavior, but no expectation *about the world* is inverted. |
| 2026-08-06 | Meeting one's dream partner, then meeting their spouse | 2/10 | MERELY-UNFORTUNATE | Heartbreak with good timing. The universe did not comment; it merely scheduled. |
| 2026-08-07 | Richmann's Rod (electrocuted replicating Franklin's kite experiment, 1753) | 6/10 | BAD-LUCK | Occupational exposure at the frontier, not a reversal — an ungrounded rod made no promise of safety, and "unfortunately" survives the substitution test. History may appeal on the ball-lightning detail. |
