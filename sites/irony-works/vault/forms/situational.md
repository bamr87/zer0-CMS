# Situational Irony

**Definition.** An outcome that reverses the expectation built into the situation itself — and, in reversing it, comments on it.

**Mechanism.** No speaker required; the world does the talking. The expectation is embedded in a plan, a design, an institution, or a promise. The reversal lands hardest when it is caused *by* the expectation: the fireproof theater that burns, the getaway car that won't start, the [[the-kodak-moment|company killed by its own invention]].

**Commonly confused with.** Bad luck and coincidence — this is the [[the-merely-unfortunate|most composted category]] in the vault. The test: could you swap in "unfortunately" without losing meaning? If yes, it's misfortune. Irony requires the outcome to *say something* about the expectation, not merely disappoint it.
