# Verbal Irony

**Definition.** Saying one thing while meaning another — usually its opposite — and trusting the listener to hear the gap.

**Mechanism.** The expectation lives in the literal sentence; the reversal lives in the delivery, the context, or the shared knowledge between speaker and audience. The meaning is the distance between the two. Sub-species: sarcasm (verbal irony with a target and teeth), understatement ("a bit of a setback," said of the Hindenburg), overstatement, and antiphrasis (nicknaming the giant "Tiny").

**Commonly confused with.** Lying. The liar wants the gap to stay hidden; the ironist wants it found. Irony fails politely when missed — lying fails catastrophically when caught.
