# Cosmic Irony

**Definition.** The universe as ironist: fate arranges outcomes as if to mock human intention specifically.

**Mechanism.** Also called irony of fate. The expectation is human striving; the reversal is delivered by forces indifferent or hostile to it, timed so precisely that indifference becomes indistinguishable from wit. Hardy built a career here. The self-defeating prophecy is its engineering diagram: the act taken to avoid the outcome *causes* the outcome (Oedipus's parents consult the oracle; the consultation sets the machinery running).

**Commonly confused with.** Ordinary tragedy. Tragedy needs only loss; cosmic irony needs *timing* — the sense that the loss was composed, not merely permitted.
