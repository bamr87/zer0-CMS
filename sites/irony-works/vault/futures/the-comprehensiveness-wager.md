# Open Position: The Comprehensiveness Wager

**The open position.** The vault bets that selective, gated, human-merged growth can scale indefinitely without tripping the [[the-borges-condition|Borges Condition]] — that an encyclopedia can approach *everything* while staying legible, traversable, and worth a reader's evening.

**Plausible reversals, enumerated without rooting for them.** (1) The archive succeeds and becomes unnavigable: comprehensiveness defeats enlightenment, and the vault becomes its own best exhibit. (2) The engine's throughput outruns human review; the gate becomes ceremonial; filler wins — the encyclopedia of irony dies of sincerity about its own quality. (3) The quiet reversal: the vault stays excellent and small, meaning the *founding mandate* reversed — perpetual growth was never the ingredient; refusal was.

**Settlement criteria.** At each maturity review the gardener records: canon size, compost ratio, orphaned-note count, and one honest paragraph on whether a newcomer starting at [[Home]] still finds a garden or a warehouse. Three consecutive warehouse verdicts settle this position as irony — and the entry migrates to [[mirrors|the Hall of Mirrors]], where a seat is being held.
