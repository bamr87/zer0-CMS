## Notable Events of 2005

| Event | Description |
|---|---|
| [Condoleezza Rice becomes Secretary of State](people/condoleezza-rice-secretary-of-state.md) | Condoleezza Rice was confirmed as U.S. Secretary of State on January 26, becoming the first female African American in the position. |
| [George W. Bush's 2005 second-term agenda](history-politics/george-w-bush-2005-agenda.md) | Bush began 2005 with ambitions for Social Security privatization and judicial reform; the year ended with his domestic agenda collapsed but the Supreme Court remade via Roberts and Alito nominations. |
| [Iraqi Parliamentary Elections](history-politics/iraqi-elections-2005.md) | Iraq held its first multi-party legislative elections since 1953 on January 30, with ~60% turnout despite insurgent threats, forming a Shia-dominated transitional assembly. |
| [Kyoto Protocol enters force](science-technology/kyoto-protocol-enters-force.md) | The international climate treaty took legal effect on February 16 for 141 signatory nations, establishing the first binding greenhouse-gas reduction targets. |
| [Huygens lands on Titan](science-technology/huygens-titan-landing.md) | ESA's Huygens probe completed the first landing in the outer solar system on January 14, transmitting 350 images from Saturn's largest moon. |
| [YouTube founded](science-technology/youtube-founded.md) | Chad Hurley, Steve Chen, and Jawed Karim registered youtube.com on February 14, 2005, launching the platform that transformed global video sharing. |
| [Hariri assassination & Cedar Revolution](history-politics/hariri-assassination-cedar-revolution.md) | A truck bomb killed former Lebanese PM Rafik Hariri on February 14, sparking mass protests that ended Syria's 29-year military presence in Lebanon. |
| [Tulip Revolution in Kyrgyzstan](history-politics/tulip-revolution-kyrgyzstan.md) | Protests over fraudulent parliamentary elections stormed government headquarters in Bishkek on March 24, ousting President Askar Akayev in Central Asia's first colour revolution. |
| [Terri Schiavo dies](society-economics/terri-schiavo-case.md) | Terri Schiavo, a 41-year-old in a persistent vegetative state, died on March 31 after a prolonged right-to-die legal battle, triggering national debate over end-of-life care and medical ethics. |
| [Pope John Paul II dies](history-politics/pope-john-paul-ii-death.md) | Karol Wojtyła, pope since 1978 and the most-traveled pontiff in history, died on April 2 after a 26-year papacy; Cardinal Ratzinger succeeded him as Benedict XVI. |
| [Pope Benedict XVI elected](history-politics/pope-benedict-xvi-2005.md) | Cardinal Joseph Ratzinger was elected Pope Benedict XVI on April 19, becoming the first German pope in over 1,000 years; his inaugural Mass on April 24 emphasized love and unity. |
| [*Star Wars* Episode III released](arts-culture/star-wars-episode-iii.md) | *Revenge of the Sith* opened May 19, completing George Lucas's prequel trilogy and grossing $848 million worldwide to close a 28-year cinematic saga. |
| [Michael Jackson acquitted](people/michael-jackson-trial-verdict.md) | Michael Jackson was acquitted on June 13 of all charges of child sexual abuse, a watershed moment in pop culture that profoundly affected his public image despite the legal victory. |
| [Live 8 concerts](arts-culture/live-8-concerts.md) | Bob Geldof and Midge Ure staged ten simultaneous benefit concerts on July 2 to press G8 leaders for African debt relief. |
| [Spain and Canada legalize same-sex marriage](society-economics/same-sex-marriage-2005.md) | Spain (July 3) and Canada (July 20) enacted marriage equality, becoming the third and fourth nations worldwide to recognise same-sex marriage. |
| [Deep Impact mission](science-technology/deep-impact-mission.md) | NASA's Deep Impact spacecraft deliberately collided with Comet Tempel 1 on July 4, marking the first active scientific excavation of a comet's interior. |
| [London wins the 2012 Olympic bid](society-economics/london-wins-2012-olympic-bid.md) | London beat Paris 54–50 in the IOC's final ballot in Singapore on July 6, becoming the first city to host the modern Summer Olympics three times. |
| [7/7 London bombings](history-politics/77-london-bombings.md) | Four coordinated suicide bomb attacks struck London's transit network on July 7, killing 52 commuters and injuring over 700 — Britain's deadliest terrorist attack since Lockerbie. |
| [Lance Armstrong wins seventh Tour de France](people/lance-armstrong-tour-de-france.md) | Lance Armstrong won a record seventh consecutive Tour de France on July 24, capping a comeback from cancer — all seven titles later stripped in 2012 for doping. |
| [Provisional IRA ends its armed campaign](history-politics/ira-ends-armed-campaign.md) | The IRA ordered an end to its armed campaign on July 28 and disarmed by September, a landmark in the Northern Ireland peace process. |
| [Discovery of Eris announced](science-technology/eris-discovery.md) | A Caltech team announced Eris (2003 UB313) on July 29, more massive than Pluto, triggering the redefinition of "planet" and Pluto's 2006 reclassification. |
| [H5N1 avian flu escalation](society-economics/avian-flu-h5n1-2005.md) | The H5N1 bird flu virus escalated from poultry to widespread human infection across Asia in 2005, infecting ~100+ people with ~40+ deaths and becoming a major pandemic threat. |
| [Israeli disengagement from Gaza](history-politics/gaza-disengagement-2005.md) | Israel evacuated all 21 Gaza settlements and 4 in the northern West Bank starting August 15, removing ~8,000 settlers and reshaping Israeli politics. |
| [Hurricane Katrina](society-economics/hurricane-katrina.md) | Category 3 at landfall on August 29, Katrina's storm surge devastated New Orleans levees, killing ~1,800 people and causing an estimated $125 billion in damage. |
| [Angela Merkel elected Chancellor](history-politics/angela-merkel-chancellor.md) | Germany elected Angela Merkel on September 18 in a closely fought contest, making her the country's first female Chancellor and leader of a grand coalition. |
| [2005 Kashmir earthquake](society-economics/kashmir-earthquake-2005.md) | A magnitude 7.6 quake struck Pakistan-administered Kashmir on October 8, killing roughly 87,000 people and leaving over 3.5 million homeless. |
| [The Colbert Report premieres](arts-culture/colbert-report-premiere.md) | Stephen Colbert's satirical news program debuted on October 17 on Comedy Central, introducing "truthiness" — the 2005 and 2006 Word of the Year — and reshaping political satire. |
| [Saddam Hussein's trial begins](history-politics/saddam-hussein-trial-begins.md) | Saddam Hussein and seven co-defendants went on trial before the Iraqi Special Tribunal on October 19 over the 1982 al-Dujail killings, in one of the decade's most closely watched cases. |
| [Rosa Parks dies](people/rosa-parks-dies.md) | Civil rights icon Rosa Parks died on October 24 aged 92; her 1955 refusal to surrender her bus seat in Montgomery catalysed the modern U.S. civil rights movement. |
| [2005 French riots](society-economics/2005-french-riots.md) | Three weeks of unrest erupted across French suburbs from October 27 after two teenagers died fleeing police, prompting a national state of emergency. |
| [Guitar Hero launches](science-technology/guitar-hero-launch.md) | Harmonix's rhythm game debuted on November 8 for PlayStation 2, featuring a guitar controller and rock soundtrack, sparking a multi-billion-dollar franchise. |
| [Xbox 360 launches](science-technology/xbox-360-launch.md) | Microsoft's Xbox 360 launched in North America on November 22, ushering in the seventh generation of video game consoles and establishing Microsoft as a major force in gaming. |
| [Ellen Johnson Sirleaf elected President of Liberia](people/ellen-johnson-sirleaf-elected.md) | Declared winner on November 23, Sirleaf became Africa's first elected female head of state, defeating George Weah with 59% in the runoff. |
| [World's first partial face transplant](science-technology/first-face-transplant.md) | Surgeons in Amiens, France, performed the first human face transplant on November 27, grafting a donor's nose, lips, and chin onto Isabelle Dinoire. |
| [*The Lion, the Witch and the Wardrobe* released](arts-culture/narnia-lion-witch-wardrobe.md) | Andrew Adamson's fantasy adaptation premiered December 9, grossing roughly $745 million worldwide and demonstrating commercial viability for literary fantasy films. |
| [*Brokeback Mountain* released](arts-culture/brokeback-mountain.md) | Ang Lee's romantic drama premiered December 9, winning three Academy Awards including Best Director and becoming a watershed film for LGBTQ+ representation. |
| [iTunes digital music sales triple](society-economics/digital-music-revolution.md) | Global digital music revenues jumped from $400 million to $1.1 billion in 2005, with Apple's iTunes capturing 80% of legal downloads and fundamentally transforming the music industry. |
