|---|---|
| [Mahmoud Abbas elected PA President]({{ '/news/people/mahmoud-abbas-elected-pa-president/' | relative_url }}) | Mahmoud Abbas won 67% of the vote on January 9 to succeed Yasser Arafat, the first Palestinian presidential election since 1996. |

| [Pope John Paul II dies]({{ '/news/history-politics/pope-john-paul-ii-death/' | relative_url }}) | Karol Wojtyła, pope since 1978 and the most-traveled pontiff in history, died on April 2 after a 26-year papacy; Cardinal Ratzinger succeeded him as Benedict XVI. |
| [Pope Benedict XVI elected]({{ '/news/history-politics/pope-benedict-xvi-2005/' | relative_url }}) | Cardinal Joseph Ratzinger was elected Pope Benedict XVI on April 19, becoming the first German pope in over 1,000 years; his inaugural Mass on April 24 emphasized love and unity. |
| [Airbus A380 makes its first flight]({{ '/news/science-technology/airbus-a380-first-flight/' | relative_url }}) | The world's largest passenger airliner completed its maiden flight from Toulouse on April 27, opening the flight-test campaign for the double-deck "superjumbo." |

| [Coldplay releases X&Y]({{ '/news/arts-culture/coldplay-x-y/' | relative_url }}) | British rock band Coldplay released their third studio album X&Y on June 6, 2005, becoming the best-selling album of the year with 8.3 million units sold globally and debuting at number one in 32 countries. |
| [Michael Jackson acquitted]({{ '/news/people/michael-jackson-trial-verdict/' | relative_url }}) | Michael Jackson was acquitted on June 13 of all charges of child sexual abuse, a watershed moment in pop culture that profoundly affected his public image despite the legal victory. |
| [MGM v. Grokster decided]({{ '/news/science-technology/grokster-decision-p2p-liability/' | relative_url }}) | The U.S. Supreme Court ruled unanimously on June 27 that P2P software distributors can be liable for actively inducing copyright infringement. |

| [Deep Impact mission]({{ '/news/science-technology/deep-impact-mission/' | relative_url }}) | NASA's Deep Impact spacecraft deliberately collided with Comet Tempel 1 on July 4, marking the first active scientific excavation of a comet's interior. |
