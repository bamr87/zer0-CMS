# Repository DNA

## 1. Concept Definition

> Machine-readable. Every generic skill, prompt, and agent reads this block to know what to grow.
> Change the `subject` (and re-derive `taxonomy`) to retarget the entire framework at a new concept.

```yaml
concept:
  subject: "<the concept, e.g. 'the year 1776'>"
  scope: "<one sentence defining what is in-scope, e.g. 'Events, people, works, and developments that occurred in or are primarily associated with <subject>.'>"
  taxonomy:
    # 4–6 categories that partition the subject. Each needs a display name and a folder slug.
    - name: "<Category One>"
      slug: "<category-one-slug>"
    - name: "<Category Two>"
      slug: "<category-two-slug>"
  source_strategy: "Verify every fact against at least two authoritative sources: one encyclopedic (Wikipedia, Britannica) and one specialist (official records, scholarly articles, established news archives) where possible. Fan sites and hobbyist blogs do not count."
  conventions:
    knowledge_table: "## Notable Events of <subject short form>"
    file_path: "<category-slug>/<topic-slug>.md"
    frontmatter:
      - title
      - date
      - category
    tone: "Factual, neutral, encyclopedic. Third person. No editorial commentary."
```
