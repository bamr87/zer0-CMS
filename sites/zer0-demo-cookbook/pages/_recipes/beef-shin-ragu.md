steps:
  - title: Brown the shin
    text: >-
      Dry the beef properly and salt it. Brown it hard in oil, in batches, until
      the surface is genuinely dark — this is the only chance the dish gets to
      build that flavour. Lift it out and keep it.
    time: 15
  - title: Build the base
    text: >-
      Drop the heat. Cook the onion, carrot and celery in the same fat with a
      pinch of salt until soft and sweet, about fifteen minutes. Add the garlic
      for a minute, then the tomato purée, and cook until it darkens.
    time: 18
  - title: Deglaze
    text: >-
      Pour in the wine, scrape everything off the base, and let it reduce until
      the smell of alcohol is gone.
    time: 5
  - title: The long part
    text: >-
      Return the beef, add the tomatoes and bay, and top up with water until the
      liquid comes two thirds up the meat. Bring to a bare simmer, lid on, and
      put it in a 150°C oven. Leave it alone for three hours.
    time: 180
  - title: Shred and reduce
    text: >-
      Lift out the beef, discard the bones, and shred the meat with two forks.
      Reduce the sauce uncovered until it clings to a spoon, then return the meat
      and season properly. It will taste better tomorrow.
    time: 20
  - title: Serve
    text: >-
      Cook the pappardelle a minute short, finish it in the sauce with a splash
      of pasta water, and serve with parmesan and parsley.
    time: 10

notes:
  - The sauce freezes better than almost anything else in this book. Freeze it without the pasta, in portions.
  - No shin? Oxtail works and is better; chuck works and is fine. Avoid anything lean.
  - Doubling this is free — the oven does not care. The browning is the only step that takes longer.
---

This is the recipe I make when I want the week to be easier. It takes twenty minutes of attention at the start, twenty at the end, and three hours in between where the oven does the work.

The only step worth being fussy about is the browning. Pale beef makes a pale ragù, and no amount of simmering recovers it.
