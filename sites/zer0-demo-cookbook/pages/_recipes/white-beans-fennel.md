---
title: "White Beans with Fennel and Lemon"
layout: recipe
cookbook: zer0-demo-cookbook
course: mains
cuisine: Mediterranean
difficulty: easy
description: "The vegetarian braise that does not taste like a substitution — fennel cooked long enough to go sweet, beans cooked long enough to go creamy."
lastmod: 2026-09-08
tags: [vegetarian, beans, one-pot]

yield:
  amount: 4
  unit: servings
  singular: serving

times:
  prep: 15
  cook: 75

equipment:
  - Heavy casserole with a lid

ingredients:
  - group: The braise
    items:
      - item: dried cannellini beans
        qty: 300
        unit: g
        note: Soaked overnight. Two 400g tins work; skip to step three.
      - item: olive oil
        qty: 4
        unit: tbsp
      - item: fennel bulbs
        singular: fennel bulb
        qty: 2
        prep: quartered, core left in
      - item: onion
        qty: 1
        prep: sliced
      - item: garlic cloves
        singular: garlic clove
        qty: 4
        prep: crushed
      - item: fennel seeds
        qty: 1
        unit: tsp
      - item: dried chilli flakes
        qty: 0.5
        unit: tsp
      - item: vegetable stock
        qty: 700
        unit: ml
  - group: To finish
    items:
      - item: lemon
        qty: 1
        prep: zest and juice
      - item: flat-leaf parsley
        qty: 0.5
        unit: cup
        prep: roughly chopped
      - item: good olive oil
        qty: 2
        unit: tbsp
        note: For pouring over at the table.

steps:
  - title: Start the beans
    text: >-
      Drain the soaked beans, cover with fresh water, bring to a hard boil for
      ten minutes, then simmer until almost tender — 40 to 60 minutes depending
      on their age. Keep the cooking water.
    time: 55
  - title: Sweeten the fennel
    text: >-
      Meanwhile, brown the fennel quarters in oil on their cut sides until they
      take real colour. Add the onion and cook until soft, then the garlic,
      fennel seeds and chilli for a minute more.
    time: 15
  - title: Braise
    text: >-
      Add the beans with enough of their water plus the stock to just cover.
      Season, bring to a tremble, cover, and cook at 160°C until the beans are
      creamy and the fennel yields to a spoon.
    time: 45
  - title: Finish
    text: >-
      Off the heat, stir through lemon zest and juice and most of the parsley.
      Let it sit for ten minutes — it thickens as it cools. Pour over the good
      oil at the table.
    time: 10

notes:
  - Salt the beans only once they are tender if they are old; young beans can be salted from the start.
  - Leftovers become soup with a splash of stock, or a bruschetta topping crushed onto toast.
---

Fennel behaves like two different vegetables depending on how long you cook it. Twenty minutes gives you something aniseedy and assertive. An hour gives you something sweet and almost jammy, which is what this dish wants.
