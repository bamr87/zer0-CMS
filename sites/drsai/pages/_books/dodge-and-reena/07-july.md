The Fourth of July was the biggest night of the year in Dusty Gulch — and it was extra special, because the Fourth of July was also Deputy Dodge's birthday! Lanterns crisscrossed Main Street, everyone wore their best red, white, and blue, and Reena and the friends had a secret surprise up their sleeves.

But all afternoon, a frightening sound kept rolling through town:

*BOOM! POP! BOOM-POP-POP!*

Everyone jumped. Tumbleweed Nubi puffed up like a furry pinecone and dove under the porch.

"The fireworks don't start until dark," said Ranger Reena. "So what's making all that noise *now*?"

"Could be trouble," said Deputy Dodge. "Let's track it down before folks get scared."

This mystery took some real detective work, because booms can come from lots of places. 

"Is it a thunderstorm?" Dodge looked up. The sky was clear blue. *Not thunder.*

"Is it blasting at the old mine?" They asked the miners. The mine was closed for the holiday. *Not the mine.*

One by one, the friends crossed off every suspect. Then Hans lifted his nose, sniffed the warm air, and smelled something *delicious.* He tugged them across the square — *POP! POP-POP!* — straight to Chef Pierre's stand, where a great iron pot sat baking in the blazing sun, its lid rattling like crazy.

Dodge bravely lifted the lid... and out fluffed a cloud of warm, white POPCORN! The summer sun had grown so hot it popped the corn kernels all by itself!

{% include components/book-plate.html src="/assets/images/books/dodge-and-reena/07-july-popcorn-mystery.jpg" alt="Chef Pierre lifts the lid of the great iron pot and warm popcorn fluffs out, solving the mystery booms" %}

"It's POPCORN!" howled Dodge, laughing so hard he sat right down. "Our scary booms were *snacks* the whole time!"

Even Nubi crept out to sniff a fluffy piece.

That night, the whole town shared big bowls of Pierre's sunshine popcorn. And when the *real* fireworks finally bloomed — BOOM! Red sparkles! WHOOSH! Gold and blue! — Nubi got frightened all over again.

So Deputy Dodge —  wrapped his soft bandana gently over Nubi's ears, and Reena held him close on a quiet hill above town, far enough away that the booms felt cozy instead of scary.

Nubi even peeked out, watching the colors dance.

Then Reena gave a little whistle — the secret signal. The whole town turned toward the hill, and the brightest firework of all burst overhead in shimmering golden letters: *HAPPY BIRTHDAY, DODGE!*

"SURPRISE!" cheered Ashley, Amr, and Tahra, as Chef Pierre came puffing up the hill carrying a towering birthday cake topped with sparklers.

{% include components/book-plate.html src="/assets/images/books/dodge-and-reena/07-july-birthday-fireworks.jpg" alt="Fireworks bloom over Dusty Gulch as Dodge wraps his bandana over Nubi's ears and Chef Pierre arrives with the sparkler birthday cake" %}

Dodge's mouth dropped open. "You did all this... for me?"

"Of course," said Reena, grinning. "The bravest deputy in the West only turns a year braver once a year!"

They sang and laughed, and the four friends munched cake and popcorn under a sky full of sparkles — the very best birthday, in the very best seats, in all of Dusty Gulch.
