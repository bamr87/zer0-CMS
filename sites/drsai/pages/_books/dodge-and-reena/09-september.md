September brought golden fields, crisp apples, and the smell of fresh-baked pies. It was time for the Dusty Gulch County Fair!

The pie contest was the grandest event of all. A long table held a dozen beautiful pies, each with a little name card: Grandma Myrna's peach, Old Walt's pumpkin, Tahra's cherry, Amr's apple, and more.

"I'm entering Hans in the Best Dog contest," said Deputy Dodge proudly. "And I'm entering Nubi in the Cleverest Pet contest," said Ranger Reena.

But just before judging — *DISASTER!* Hans spotted a hot-dog stand and *zoomed* under the pie table! It wobbled, and every single name card went *flutter-flutter-flutter* into one big jumbled pile on the ground.

{% include components/book-plate.html src="/assets/images/books/dodge-and-reena/09-september-pie-mixup.jpg" alt="Hans bursts from under the pie table as every name card flutters into a jumbled pile at the county fair" %}

"Oh no," gasped Tahra. "Now nobody knows whose pie is whose — and judging starts any minute!"

"A mix-up mystery," said Reena. "But I know exactly who can solve it." She turned to Hans. "Use your nose, boy!"

Hans took a giant sniff of the first pie — *SNUFFLE* — then trotted straight to Old Walt and woofed. "That IS my pumpkin pie!" said Walt. Correct!

One by one, Hans matched each pie to its baker: peach to Grandma Myrna, cherry to Tahra, apple to Amr. *Snuffle, woof, snuffle, woof.*

But then he reached two pies that smelled almost exactly the same — both berry, both purple, both sweet. Hans sniffed one. Sniffed the other. Sniffed again, and looked confused.

"They're too alike!" said Dodge. "Now what?"

Reena leaned in close and spotted the tiniest clue. "Wait — this one has a little braided crust, and *that* one has a smooth crust. Ashley always braids hers!" Mystery within a mystery, solved.

And one final card was missing altogether — until Nubi pawed at Hans's furry tail, where the last name card was stuck the whole time! Everyone roared with laughter.

When every pie was matched at last, the judges gave Hans a brand-new blue ribbon: **"Best Nose at the Fair."** And Nubi won his own contest, for being the quickest, cleverest cat around.

{% include components/book-plate.html src="/assets/images/books/dodge-and-reena/09-september-best-nose-ribbon.jpg" alt="Hans proudly wears his blue “Best Nose at the Fair” ribbon, surrounded by happy bakers holding their pies" %}

"We BOTH won!" cheered Dodge, hugging his good (and slightly sticky) dog.

That evening, the friends shared warm slices of every pie at once, watching the sun set gold over the fairgrounds.

"Best fair ever," said Reena.

"Even with the great pie pile-up," laughed Dodge.
