Snow was melting in Dusty Gulch, and pink paper hearts were popping up all over town. Valentine's Day was coming!

When Deputy Dodge opened his door that morning, a valentine fluttered down at his feet — a big, wobbly red one. But there was no name on it. Only a riddle:

*"To find your friend, just follow my clue: begin where the water runs cold, deep, and blue."*

"A secret valentine!" gasped Dodge. "But who would send a riddle?"

He ran to find Ranger Reena — and discovered she'd gotten one too, on *her* doorstep, with the very same riddle!

{% include components/book-plate.html src="/assets/images/books/dodge-and-reena/02-february-riddle-hearts.jpg" alt="Dodge and Reena each hold a big red valentine heart with the mystery riddle, hearts strung across the street behind them" %}

"Cold, deep, and blue water," said Reena. "That's the old well in the square. Let's go!"

At the well, tied to the bucket rope, swayed a second heart: *"Now trot to the place where the sweet smells drift, where Pierre flips a treat with a flick and a lift."*

"The crepe stand!" they shouted together.

Chef Pierre only smiled and pointed his spatula at a third heart, stuck to his sugar jar. That clue sent them to the barn. The barn clue sent them to the big oak tree. Each step, Hans sniffed the trail and Nubi pounced on the next hidden heart.

But the riddles were tricky, and twice they guessed wrong. One clue said *"where horses rest their sleepy heads"* — Dodge ran to the stable, but the heart was actually at the hitching post out front! Another sent them in a circle right back to the well.

"Somebody clever made these," puffed Reena. "I bet it's Tahra. She loves riddles."

"Or Amr," said Dodge. "Or Ashley!"

The very last clue led them under the big oak tree at sunset. They tiptoed closer... and —

*"SURPRISE!"*

Out jumped Ashley, Amr, AND Tahra — all three, giggling. They'd made the secret valentine *together*, each writing one tricky riddle.

"We wanted you to have an adventure," said Tahra, "because you give everyone else so many!"

In the middle of the group hung one enormous heart, signed by every friend.

{% include components/book-plate.html src="/assets/images/books/dodge-and-reena/02-february-oak-tree-surprise.jpg" alt="Ashley, Amr, and Tahra spring their surprise under the big oak tree, one enormous valentine hanging from its branches" %}

Then came the twist: Reena reached into her satchel and pulled out a secret valentine of her *own* — for Dodge. And Dodge, blushing, pulled one out for Reena. They'd each been planning a surprise too!

Everyone laughed until their sides hurt.

"To my best friend," read Reena's heart. "Happiest trails, always."

"Best friends forever and ever," read Dodge's.

Hans woofed. Nubi purred. And Dusty Gulch glowed with love — one tricky riddle, and one paper heart, at a time.
