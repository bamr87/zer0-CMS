May arrived with warm sunshine and fields of wildflowers, and the whole town gathered for the May Day Hoedown. A tall ribbon-pole stood in the square, and the snack table groaned with cornbread, berry muffins, and Chef Pierre's famous blueberry pie.

But there was trouble. Last year, a sneaky snack thief had robbed the whole table — and this year, the town was determined to catch it.

"My cornbread's already gone!" hollered Old Walt.

"And a muffin!" cried Ashley.

Everyone turned to look at Hans. Hans sat up very straight and looked deeply offended. *"Woof!"* (He hadn't touched a single crumb.)

"This is the work of the Masked Marauder," said Deputy Dodge gravely. "And this year, we catch him."

Reena studied the table for clues. "Look — the snack box latch is open, but it wasn't broken. Whoever did this *knows how to open it.* That's one clever bandit."

So the friends set a trap. They latched the box tight and hid behind a hay bale to watch. Sure enough — *sneak, sneak, sneak* — a furry face appeared, with a black mask of fur right across its eyes. A raccoon! With nimble little fingers, it flipped the latch *click* and reached inside.

{% include components/book-plate.html src="/assets/images/books/dodge-and-reena/05-may-raccoon-snack-thief.jpg" alt="The masked raccoon reaches into the snack box as the friends catch him in the act at the festival" %}

"There he is!" cried Dodge — and the chase was ON! Around the ribbon-pole, through the dancers, over a barrel — *yeeha!* — Hans bounding, Nubi zipping, the raccoon scampering up a post with a muffin in its paws.

But Reena didn't chase. She *thought.* "He's not bad," she said. "He's just hungry and clever. Chasing him will only make him sneakier. We need to outsmart him with kindness."

So the friends built the Masked Marauder his very own snack box — one with a tricky little puzzle door that opened just for him, set at the far edge of the square, filled with treats.

The clever raccoon figured out the puzzle in no time, *click-click*, and happily munched his own muffin — leaving the town's table alone at last.

"You didn't catch the bandit," said Old Walt, amazed. "You trained him!"

With the mystery solved, everyone grabbed a ribbon and danced around the pole, weaving a rainbow. Dodge held yellow, Reena held pink, and they spun until they were dizzy and laughing.

{% include components/book-plate.html src="/assets/images/books/dodge-and-reena/05-may-maypole-puzzle-box.jpg" alt="Dancers weave rainbow ribbons around the maypole while the raccoon happily munches a muffin from his own puzzle-door snack box" %}

"Best May Day ever," puffed Dodge.

"Because the cleverest thief in Dusty Gulch is finally on our side," said Reena.
