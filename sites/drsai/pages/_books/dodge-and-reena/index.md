**Deputy Dodge & Ranger Reena: Adventures of a Lifetime** follows the bravest young deputy in the West, his best friend Ranger Reena, and their animal companions through one full year in Dusty Gulch — a friendly old-west desert town of wooden storefronts, red-rock mesas, and big turquoise skies. Every month brings a small, kind mystery: wiggly tracks in the snow, a masked snack thief, a star that will not shine. Every mystery ends warm.

## Meet the friends

- **Deputy Dodge** — young blonde cowboy, blue eyes, oversized tan hat, red bandana, denim shirt, gold star badge.
- **Ranger Reena** — young ranger girl, light-brown skin, brown eyes, dark curly pigtails, olive flat-brim hat, green shirt, olive vest, pink accents.
- **Thunderpaws Hans** — giant rideable dog, caramel back, white belly, pointy ears.
- **Nubi** — small sandy-orange tabby cat (he), cream belly and paws, curly tumbleweed tail; rides on Reena.
- **Milky Way** — small creamy dappled pony, turquoise saddle blanket.
- **Chef Pierre** — cheerful French chef, no mustache, white toque and jacket, tricolor neckerchief, blue crepe cart.
- **Ashley** — artistic young woman, blonde braid, pink gingham dress, sun bonnet.
- **Amr** — young man, light-brown skin, dark hair pulled back, plaid shirt, suspenders, pageboy cap.
- **Tahra** — young woman, olive skin, long dark wavy hair with a bright ribbon, embroidered teal-accented vest.

## How this book is made

This book is the test content for the [drsai children's book platform](https://github.com/bamr87/drsai). The stories live in the [Dodge-and-Reena-Book](https://github.com/bamr87/Dodge-and-Reena-Book) repo and are imported here by `tools/import_dodge_and_reena.py`. Each story page carries the exact image prompts that produced its illustrations; missing scenes are rendered with the OpenAI Images API by `tools/generate_illustrations.py`, using the shared style and character bible so the cast looks the same on every page.
