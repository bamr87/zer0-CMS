In a town called Mixlington, lively and grand,
Lived a toddler named Reena, with plans in her hand.
She started at daycare, where fun was the rule,
With blocks and with songs, it was better than school.
But home with her kitty, sweet Nubi the cat,
Reena bossed him around, saying "Sit! And like that!"
She'd point with her finger, so tiny and bold,
Commanding poor Nubi to fetch or to hold.
Now Reena mixed words in a whimsical way,
Blending Arabic phrases with English each day.
She'd say "Habibi cat, come play with the ball!"
Or "Shukran for cuddles, you're fluffy and tall!"
A creature named Zippity-Zong came to play,
Invented by Reena in her special way.
It taught her that mixing makes languages sing,
And bossing with kindness lets friendships take wing!

---
Moral: And bossing with kindness lets friendships take wing!
