Once upon an algorithm…

drsai is a children's book platform where AI helps tell the story: verses and tales from a story engine, watercolor worlds from image models, and a bookshelf that lives on GitHub Pages.

How a book gets made

1. The words

Stories are drafted with the drsai story engine (OpenAI-powered, with meter and rhyme validation for verse) and refined by hand in their own content repo — Dodge-and-Reena-Book is the first.

2. The pictures

Every story carries its illustration prompts in front matter — a shared style and character bible keeps the cast consistent. tools/generate_illustrations.py renders missing scenes with the OpenAI Images API.

3. The book

The zer0-mistakes theme's books collection turns it all into a picture book: cover, table of contents, and immersive story pages — rendered right here on GitHub Pages.
