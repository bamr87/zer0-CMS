# Read a site's voice and test a text against it — evidence

> **Feature:** `ZER0-046` · **Scenario:** `verify/scenarios/studio-voice-resolve.yml` · **Generated by:** `node verify/runner.mjs --only studio-voice-resolve` against `http://127.0.0.1:8000` on 2026-10-31, the app started as `verify/verify.yml` says (a fresh database, `seed_demo`, the recorded provider)

An editor opens the demo site's Voice tab and reads, without a model, what its voice is and who is told it: on a fresh seed the site has one layer — the guard's built-in lists, 13 bans and 5 warnings in force, 4 rules the guard never applies shown as skipped — read by the four gates and `brand.rules`, while AIEO's agents, book tasks and the editor's AI panel are named as never reached; and 0 of the 40 assistants the editor may run there list `brand.rules`, so none of them is told the voice. A sentence with "cutting-edge" in it, tested under that voice, gets the guard's verdict at once: one error, the guard would refuse it. The page never scrolls sideways at either width; the map scrolls inside its own box at a phone's.
